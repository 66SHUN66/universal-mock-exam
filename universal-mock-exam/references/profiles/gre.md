# GRE General Test — exam profile

> This profile stores verified facts plus explicitly estimated structure. Anything marked `[estimated]` is inference, not official ETS guidance.

## 1. Facts (ETS-published)

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sections | Analytical Writing (1 task), Verbal Reasoning (2 sections), Quantitative Reasoning (2 sections) |
| Timing | AWA: 1 "Analyze an Issue" essay, 30 min. Verbal: 12 questions / 18 min, then 15 questions / 23 min. Quant: 12 questions / 21 min, then 15 questions / 26 min. Total test time about 1 h 58 min |
| Adaptive design | Section-level adaptive: the difficulty of the second Verbal and second Quant section depends on performance in the first |
| Scoring | Verbal 130–170, Quantitative 130–170 (1-point increments); Analytical Writing 0–6 in half-point increments |
| Score validity | 5 years |
| Delivery | Computer-delivered at a test centre or at home; unofficial Verbal/Quant scores shown at the end of the test, AWA arrives later |
| Format history | Shortened in September 2023: the unscored experimental section and the second essay task were removed, question counts and timing dropped accordingly |

## 3. High-frequency content

**Verbal**
1. Reading comprehension: primary purpose, inference, author's attitude, detail selection, logical structure
2. Text completion: single blank, double blank, triple blank; connector words decide the direction
3. Sentence equivalence: pick two options producing the same meaning
4. Vocabulary is tested inside context, not as isolated word lists

**Quantitative**
1. Arithmetic: ratios, percentages, rates, averages, integer properties, remainders, primes
2. Algebra: linear and quadratic equations, inequalities, functions, exponent rules
3. Geometry: triangles, circles, coordinate geometry, area and volume, inscribed figures
4. Data analysis: mean/median/mode, standard deviation comparison, probability, counting, sets, graph and table reading
5. Quantitative comparison: the same four answer choices every time, so estimation beats full computation

**AWA**
1. Analyze an Issue: take a position and defend it with specific examples
2. Graded on the quality of reasoning and control of language, not on word count

## 4. Scoring and grading

- Verbal and Quant are right/wrong per question, but the raw score is mapped through equating, so a single question is not worth a fixed number of points. A mock paper therefore reports a **converted 130–170 estimate plus a raw correct count**, and states that the conversion is approximate.
- AWA is scored 0–6 on a holistic rubric; a mock grader should return the rubric dimensions (task response, reasoning, organisation, language control) rather than a single number.
- Percentiles are published yearly by ETS and shift between years. Never hardcode a percentile table into the paper; cite the current ETS percentile document with its date.

## 5. Intelligence status

- Official facts (sections, timing, score range, adaptive design): verifiable, cite ETS.
- Per-section item counts after the 2023 change: ETS publishes approximate counts, exact counts vary by form → treat as approximate.
- Third-party prep content is abundant (books, question banks, video walkthroughs) and largely agrees on strategy, but almost all of it reproduces copyrighted questions. This skill must generate original items in the same **style**, never copy retired questions.
